⚠ SET BUCK TO 5V FIRST

Before connecting ESP32, power buck from batteries. Use a multimeter on OUT+ / OUT−. Turn the trimmer pot until you read exactly 5.0V. Then connect everything else.

⚠ CAP POLARITY

Long leg (+) → 5V rail. Short leg / stripe (−) → GND. Reversed polarity will burst the capacitor. The white stripe on the body marks the negative side.

📡 I²C SHARED WIRES

OLED and RTC share the same SDA (GPIO 21) and SCL (GPIO 22) wires. One pair of wires branches to both. Different addresses (0x3C / 0x68) — no conflict.

🔌 3.3V DISTRIBUTION

ESP32's 3.3V output pin powers BB2's + rail via a jumper wire. OLED and RTC must use 3.3V — never connect them to 5V directly or they will be damaged.

✅ 10kΩ PULL-UP WIRING

GPIO 34 & 35 have no internal pull-up. Each needs a 10kΩ resistor between its GPIO pin and 3.3V. Button Leg A → GPIO. Button Leg B → GND.
